# Witchcraft

**Become a witch. Cast spells. Curse your enemies. Command the ancient arts.**

---

Witchcraft isn't just another magic plugin — it's a way of life. One where every spell feels earned, every ritual matters, and the line between power and consequence is razor thin.

## What's Inside

### Ritual Cauldron Magic
Drop ingredients into a cauldron and watch as dark energy crackles to life. No instant casts. No shortcuts. Just you, the cauldron, and the old ways. Mix the wrong ingredients? Things might get ugly.

### Spoken Incantations
Learn ancient phrases and whisper them into the void. Type the words in chat and feel the magic take hold. But beware — some incantations are not meant for mortal tongues.

### 28 Unique Spells
From cursing your rivals with misfortune and famine, to summoning allies from across the world — there's a spell for every situation. Blesss your farmland, shield yourself from arrows, peer into another player's soul, or banish them to the void entirely.

### Curses That Stick
Curse someone's crops. Jinx their fishing. Blind them. Silence them. Make their phantom nightmares worse. The best part? They won't know it was you... unless they find a way to lift it.

### Protection & Wards
Shield yourself from curses, block scrying attempts, gain fire resistance, or deflect projectiles. Wards don't last forever though — stay vigilant.

### Scrying
Peer through the veil and learn another player's secrets. Where they are. What they're holding. Whether a storm is brewing over their head. Or dowse for hidden treasure nearby.

### Target Paper
Write a name on paper at an anvil, drop it in the cauldron, and the spell finds its mark. Simple. Elegant. Terrifying.

## How It Works

1. **Fill a cauldron** with water
2. **Drop in the right ingredients** — each spell has its own recipe
3. **Optionally add a target paper** if the spell affects another player
4. **Stand near and wait** — the ritual charges with swirling particles
5. **Done.** The magic does the rest.

Or just **learn an incantation** and type it in chat. No cauldron needed.
